ORDER
Challenging the impugned Order passed by the trial Court in I.A.No.9 of 
2025 in O.S.No.8986 of 2025, the plaintiffs have preferred the present Civil 
Revision Petition.  
2.  The petitioners/plaintiffs have filed the suit for declaration to declare 
that the set back area as a common area described in the suit schedule property 
which is exclusively belonging to the plaintiff and for permanent and mandatory 
injunctions.  Pending suit, the plaintiffs have filed an application for amendment 
of  the  plaint.   The  respondents  have  raised  objections  by  filing  counter. 
Considering the submissions on either side, the trial Court had dismissed the 
application.   Aggrieved over the same, the present Civil Revision Petition has 
been filed by the plaintiffs.

3.  The learned counsel appearing for the petitioners argues that though 
the corrections relate to only measurements, schedule descriptions and case 
numbers already involved are in the pleadings and the same do not substitute a 
different factual matrix or releif.  But the trial Court failed to consider the same 
and dismissed the application.   Hence, prayed to set aside the Order of the trial 
Court.
4.  A perusal of records reveal that through proposed amendment, the 
extent  is not  changed and only the manner in which the measurement  is 
calculated alone is changed and particularly, the amendment will not change the 
character of the suit.   If the amendment is allowed it would not cause any 
prejudice  to  the  respondents  and  it  would  only  avoid  multiplicity  of 
proceedings.  Now the suit is pending at the stage of trial and posted for 
examination of P.W.1.  Hence, this Court is inclined to allow this revision 
petition.      
5.  Accordingly, this Civil Revision Petition is allowed and the Order of 
the trial Court in I.A.No.9 of 2025 in O.S.No.8986 of 2021 is set aside.   No 
costs.   Consequently, connected miscellaneous petition is closed.
